# Dataset card: NINA thermal v1 (frp20000)

The dataset behind the paper's thermal model (Table 5). The images are not in git
(publication outside git is pending, #17). The versioned description is in
`../data/manifests/thermal_v1_*`, generated by
`training/pipelines/run_dataset_manifests.py`.

## Composition

One image per patch, degradation profile B, fixed AGC. Splits are granule-disjoint.

| split | frames | granules | fire | no-fire | fire rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | 219 | 72 | 67 | 152 | 0.306 |
| val | 36 | 11 | 17 | 19 | 0.472 |
| test | 82 | 21 | 36 | 46 | 0.439 |

Pool: 924 unique patches from 201 granules (4 data roots merged, 171 duplicates removed;
`data_202408/` repeats August 2024 from `data/`). If roots disagree on a granule's split,
it is held out everywhere (test > val > train). After FRP relabelling: 120 fire, 217 no-fire,
587 ambiguous (excluded).

Granule sensing months: 2023-06 (42 granules), 2023-08 (40), 2024-08 (41), 2024-09 (39),
2025-09 (39).

## Sources

- **Imagery:** Sentinel-3 SLSTR Level-1B radiances (`SL_1_RBT`). They are converted to brightness
  temperature, resampled to the 120 × 160 Lepton geometry (640 × 480 km footprint), and
  degraded with sensor profiles A/B/C (`datagen/config.py` `DEGRADATION_PROFILES`, paper
  Table 2).
- **Labels:** NASA FIRMS, VIIRS S-NPP 375 m active fires, standard-processing product (SP).
  Queried bbox lon −67.5…−59.7, lat −20.5…−11.8 (`thermal_v1_firms_query.json`). The NRT
  CSVs in the folders are header-only and add no detections.

## Labelling (D-023)

- **fire:** summed FRP over the footprint ≥ 20,000 MW within ±1 day of sensing
- **no-fire:** zero FIRMS detections in the footprint
- **ambiguous (excluded):** anything in between

## Limitations

1. **June 2023 has no FIRMS data.** All 42 June 2023 granules therefore have "zero
   detections", and their patches became no-fire by construction. They supply 138 of
   152 train negatives, 17 of 19 val negatives and **all 46 test negatives**. Every
   positive is from August or September. The issue text "FIRMS Aug–Sep 2023–2025"
   describes the FIRMS coverage correctly, but the dataset also contains June 2023.
2. **Narrow FIRMS bbox.** Patch footprints extend well beyond the queried box. Zero
   detections often means "not queried" rather than "no fire". In the later relabelling,
   70% of v1 no-fire patches lay entirely outside it.
3. **Warm-scene confound.** Large fire complexes occur on hot, dry days. The label cannot
   separate fire activity from scene warmth at 20 GW.
4. **Small sample.** 337 retained frames, 104 granules.

Limitations 1 and 2 are fixed in the v3/v4 datasets (wide bbox, full date coverage,
coverage guards, low-activity negatives). These are documented in
`docs/TRAINING_REPORT_v3.md` §2.2 (pending merge to `main`).
